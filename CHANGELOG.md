# Changelog

## Unreleased

<!-- One line per user-visible change, in plain words. -->
- MirrorMap Dash can send your Dash Cam link to the chats you pick.
- Calls from the watch: call a text, WhatsApp, Signal or Telegram chat from the watch's reply menu. The call starts on your phone. Turn it on in Settings.
- Reactions picked on the watch always go as reactions, and emoji picked to write always go as text.
- When a message or reaction sent from the watch doesn't go, the watch shows a notification saying so.
- Pairing Google Messages can always be cancelled or started over, and a pairing that runs out no longer leaves the app stuck on the confirm screen.
- After you log out of every network, the watch no longer shows the old chats, and logging out reaches an open watch app right away.
- Google Messages pairing asks your phone the way the Google Messages website does, and if your Google account uses Google Messages on more than one phone, Start over asks the next one.
- Start a new chat from the watch with someone on Google Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal or Discord: New chat at the top of the watch's account switcher lists your contacts, and saying or typing part of a name finds them.
- The Emoji substitution setting shows only for the Pebble Time and Time Steel. The Time 2 shows every emoji as sent.
- Google Messages pairing no longer stops with "Pairing didn't finish" right after the emoji appears.
- Image quality is now one setting, from Faster to Sharper. Tall pictures no longer come out squashed.
- When MirrorMap or MirrorMov is open on a Pebble Time 2, a new message shows as a small card inside it instead of closing it (needs MirrorMap Dash). Tap the card to open the chat; Back from the chat takes you back to the map.
